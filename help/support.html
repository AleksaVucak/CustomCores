Consultation & support guide

This guide explains how to request a personalised PC consultation (with optional file attachments), track our responses, leave a product review, and send a general contact message. Use the table of contents to jump to the task you are on.

Ways to get help

CustomCore offers three support channels, depending on what you need:

  • PC consultation — tell us your budget and goals and get a tailored build recommendation (requires an account).
  • Product reviews — share your experience of a system and help other shoppers (requires an account).
  • Contact us — a general message about anything at all (open to guests too).

Requesting a consultation

Open Request a PC consultation (you must be logged in). Tell us how you plan to use your PC and we will put together a recommendation. Required fields are marked with an asterisk.

  • Approximate budget — required; choose a range from the list.
  • Games you play — required (up to 2000 characters), e.g. “Cyberpunk 2077 at 1440p”.
  • Other software you use — required (up to 2000 characters), e.g. Blender, OBS, or “none — gaming only”.
  • Performance goals — required (up to 2000 characters), e.g. “144+ FPS at 1440p, quiet operation”.
  • Additional notes — optional (up to 2000 characters) for brands, aesthetics, or deadlines.
  • Attachments — optional reference files (see below).

Choose Submit request. On success you get a confirmation with your request number and how many files were attached. If a field needs fixing, the form reloads with the problems highlighted and your text preserved.

Adding attachments

You can attach optional reference files to a consultation — screenshots, part lists, or quotes. The limits are:

  • File types: PDF, TXT, PNG, JPG, and WEBP.
  • Count: up to 5 files per request.
  • Size: about 2 MB per file (the exact limit is shown on the form).

For security, each file is checked by its real content type (not just its name), stored under a generated name, and kept in a directory that cannot be browsed directly. If an upload is rejected, fix the issue and — because browsers do not keep file selections — re-select your files before submitting again.

Your attachments are private to your account. You (and our team) can download them later from your consultation history; other customers can never see them.

Tracking your requests

Open Consultation history to see every request you have made — and only your own. Each card shows the request number, the date submitted, its current status, the details you provided, any attachments (with secure download links), and our response once it arrives.

Use the status filter (All, plus each status) to narrow the list, or choose New request to start another consultation.

Our responses & statuses

When our team replies, the response appears on the request in your history, along with the date it was answered. Until then you will see a note that we have not responded yet. Each request carries a status:

  • Open — received; waiting for our team (the status of a new request).
  • In progress — we are working on your recommendation.
  • Answered — we have posted a response.
  • Closed — the request is complete.

Writing a review

The Reviews page lists approved reviews (optionally filtered to one product) with an average rating. To write one you must be logged in; you can start from the Reviews page or from any product page.

  • Product — pick the system you are reviewing (already set when you start from a product page).
  • Rating — required, 1–5 stars.
  • Title — required (up to 200 characters).
  • Your review — required, at least 20 characters.

New reviews are saved as pending and stay private until an administrator approves them, at which point they appear publicly. You can submit one review per product; if you already have one, its status is shown instead of the form.

Contacting us

Use Contact us for any general question. Guests and signed-in customers are both welcome — if you are logged in, your name and email are filled in from your account. The form asks for:

  • Your name — required (up to 200 characters).
  • Email address — required and valid (up to 255 characters); we reply here.
  • Subject — required; pick a topic, or choose Other and type your own.
  • Message — required, at least 10 characters (up to 5000). Include order numbers or product names where relevant.

Choose Send message. You will get a confirmation with a reference number and the email address we will reply to. For a build recommendation, use a consultation instead.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • Can’t open the consultation form? — Consultations require you to log in; contact us is open to guests.
  • Attachment rejected. — Check the file type (PDF, TXT, PNG, JPG, WEBP), keep each under the size shown, and no more than 5 files; then re-select and resubmit.
  • Review not showing. — Reviews stay pending until approved; you can only post one per product.
  • Session expired message. — Reload the page and submit again; this protects the forms against cross-site request forgery.
  • No response yet? — Check consultation history; we email you when there is an update.
  • Wrong channel? — Use a consultation for build advice, a review to rate a system, and contact for everything else.
