Catalogue & products guide

This guide explains how to browse the catalogue by performance tier, search and filter systems, read a product page and its configuration options, compare systems side by side, keep a wishlist, and leave reviews. Use the table of contents to jump to the task you are on.

How the catalogue works

The catalogue lists every active CustomCore system, loaded live from the database. Systems are organised into four performance tiers, Budget, Esports, High-Performance, and Creator: so you can compare options within a category.

At the top of the catalogue, Catalogue at a glance shows a bar chart of how many active products sit in each tier, with the price range for each. If the chart cannot load, the same figures appear in a table underneath, and each tier name links straight to that tier’s products.

Open the catalogue

Browsing & tiers

Use the tier bar (All, Budget, Esports, High-Performance, Creator) to switch between categories. The toolbar shows how many systems match your current view, and each tier can include a short description.

Every product card shows:

  • The product image and name (both link to the full product page).
  • Its performance tier and a short summary.
  • Key default specs, processor, graphics, and memory.
  • The starting price (“From $…”) and stock status, plus a Featured badge where applicable.
  • Add to compare: tick 2 to 4 cards, then choose Compare selected (see Comparing systems).
  • View details to open the product page.

Searching

Use the search box on the catalogue or the dedicated search page. A search matches across product name, brand, description, tier name, and key specs (for example the CPU or GPU), and returns up to 50 systems.

  • Try a tier name (Budget, Esports, High-Performance, Creator), a brand, or part of a GPU/CPU label such as RTX or Ryzen.
  • Search terms are limited to 100 characters, and special characters are treated literally.
  • If nothing matches, you will see a message with suggestions and a link to browse the full catalogue.

Filtering

The Filters panel on the catalogue narrows the list. Filters work individually and combine together:

  • Category: limit to a single performance tier.
  • Brand: limit to one brand.
  • Price range: set a minimum and/or maximum price (steps of $50).
  • In stock only: hide systems that are out of stock.

Choose Apply to run the filters. When any filter is active, a Clear all link appears to reset to the full catalogue. Your active filters are summarised in the toolbar above the results, and they stay in the page URL so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

Sorting

Use Sort by in the Filters panel to reorder the results:

  • Featured (default), featured systems first, then lowest price.
  • Price: low → high and Price: high → low.
  • Name: A → Z and Name: Z → A.
  • Newest first.

The current order is shown in the toolbar as “Sorted by …”.

The product page

Selecting a system opens its product page, which includes:

  • A breadcrumb (Catalogue / tier / product) and the product image.
  • The starting price (“From $…”); if the default options add cost, the base price is shown too.
  • Live stock status and a full description.
  • A Default specifications table (processor, graphics, memory, storage, category, brand).
  • Configuration options, an add-to-cart form, a wishlist button, and reviews.
  • Footer links back to the tier, to compare this system, and to all systems.

Configuration options & pricing

Many systems offer configuration options grouped by type (for example memory, storage, or colour). Each group is a set of choices:

  • One option per group is pre-selected as the default.
  • Each choice shows its effect on price, +$…, −$…, or Included when there is no change.
  • The “From” price on the page reflects the default selections; changing options changes the total.

Pick the options you want, set a quantity, and choose Add to cart. Out-of-stock systems show a notice instead of the add-to-cart button. Prices are always recalculated on the server from the database, so the amount in your cart is trusted. See the Cart & orders guide for what happens next.

Comparing systems

Compare puts 2 to 4 systems side by side. You can build a comparison two ways:

  • On the catalogue, tick Add to compare on each card, then choose Compare selected.
  • On the compare page, tick systems in the picker and choose Compare selected.

The comparison table lines up the same fields for every system, brand, category, base price, availability, processor, graphics, memory, storage, featured status, and summary. Use Remove above a column to drop a system, or Clear selection to start over. You can compare a maximum of four systems at once; the selection lives in the page URL, so a comparison is easy to share or bookmark.

Wishlist

A wishlist lets you save products to buy later. You must be logged in, on a product page choose ♡ Save to wishlist (guests see a prompt to log in).

Open Wishlist from your account to manage saved items:

  • Move to cart: adds the product with its default configuration (price recalculated on the server) and removes it from the wishlist.
  • Remove: takes a single item off the wishlist.
  • Clear wishlist: removes everything (with a confirmation prompt).

Each product appears on your wishlist at most once, and your wishlist is private to your account. Items that are no longer available show “Currently unavailable” with a link to view the product instead of a move-to-cart button.

Reviews

Product pages show approved customer reviews with an average star rating, and link to the full reviews page. To write a review you must be logged in; then, on the product page:

  • Choose a rating (1 to 5 stars), required.
  • Add a title: required.
  • Write your review: required, at least 20 characters.

New reviews are saved as pending and stay private until an administrator approves them, at which point they appear publicly. You can submit one review per product; if you already have one, its status (pending, approved, or hidden) is shown instead of the form.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • No results after filtering?: Use Clear all to reset, then apply one filter at a time.
  • “Compare selected” did nothing?: You need at least two systems ticked, and no more than four.
  • Price looks higher than “From”.: Some options add cost; the total updates as you change them.
  • Can’t save to a wishlist or write a review?: Both require you to log in.
  • Review not visible yet?: Reviews stay pending until an administrator approves them.
  • Out of stock?: You cannot add the system to your cart until stock returns; save it to your wishlist to check back later.
