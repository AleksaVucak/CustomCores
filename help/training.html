End-user training walkthrough

New to CustomCore? Follow this numbered walkthrough from creating an account through shopping or building, placing an order, and reviewing your first purchase. Each step links into the live site and to the detailed guide for that area.

Start here

This walkthrough takes about ten minutes and covers the whole journey:

  1. Create an account and sign in.
  2. Shop the catalogue or build a custom PC.
  3. Add to cart and check out.
  4. Track your order and leave a review.

Good to know: you can browse the catalogue and try the PC Builder as a guest, but saving builds, using a wishlist, checking out, and leaving reviews all require a free account. CustomCore is an academic demo, checkout is simulated and never collects real payment details.

Step 1, Create your account

  1. Open Create account and enter your name, email, and a password (at least 8 characters). A phone number is optional.
  2. Choose Create account. When it succeeds you are taken to the login page.
  3. Open Log in and sign in with your email and password. You land on your account dashboard.

Full details, including editing your profile, changing your password, and session timeouts, are in the Accounts & profile guide.

Step 2, Shop or build

You have two ways to choose a PC. Pick whichever suits you, you can do both.

Option A, Shop a prebuilt system

  1. Open the catalogue and use the tier bar, filters, sorting, or search to find a system.
  2. Select View details to open a product, read its specs, and pick any configuration options, the price updates with your choices.
  3. Not sure? Tick Add to compare on 2 to 4 systems and choose Compare selected to see them side by side.

More detail is in the Catalogue & products guide (browsing, filters, options, comparing, wishlist, and reviews).

Option B, Build your own

Prefer a fully custom PC? Jump to Step 2b, Build a custom PC.

Step 2b, Build a custom PC

  1. Open the PC Builder and work through the steps (CPU, motherboard, GPU, RAM, storage, PSU, case, cooling, operating system, and optional assembly service).
  2. Watch the running total, live compatibility status, and performance preview as you choose parts. Resolve any incompatibility errors before finishing.
  3. Choose Review build to see the full summary. If you are logged in, give the build a name and save it to your account.

Every step, plus pricing, compatibility, the performance chart, and saved builds, is explained in the PC Builder guide.

Step 3, Place an order

  1. Add your chosen system, product, or saved build to the cart. Adjust quantities if needed, the subtotal updates as you go.
  2. Choose Proceed to checkout. Your shipping details are pre-filled from your profile where possible; complete any required fields.
  3. Pick a (simulated) payment method and choose Place order.
  4. You will see an order confirmation with a unique number in the form CC-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX, keep it for your records.

The cart, checkout fields, payment methods, and confirmation are covered in the Cart & orders guide.

Step 4, After your order

  1. Open order history to see your orders and their status, and select one to view its full details.
  2. Once you have used a system, leave a review, choose a rating, add a title, and write at least a couple of sentences. Reviews are published after an administrator approves them.
  3. Want tailored advice for a future build? Request a consultation with your budget and goals, and track the reply in your consultation history.

Order statuses and receipts are in the Cart & orders guide; reviews, consultations, and contacting us are in the Consultation & support guide.
