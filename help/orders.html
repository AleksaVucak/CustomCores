Cart & orders guide

This guide explains how to add products and custom builds to your cart, adjust quantities, complete the simulated checkout, read your order confirmation number, and track orders in your history. Use the table of contents to jump to the step you are on.

How ordering works

Ordering has three steps: add items to your cart, complete checkout with your shipping and payment choice, and receive an order confirmation. You can then follow the order in your order history.

You need an account. The cart, checkout, and order pages require you to be logged in. Your cart is saved to your account, so it is still there when you log back in.

Simulated store. CustomCore is an academic demo — checkout does not collect or process real payment card details.

Your cart

Add items to your cart in two ways:

  • Catalogue products — on a product page, choose any configuration options and a quantity, then select Add to cart.
  • Saved builds — from a saved build, add your custom PC to the cart (custom builds are limited to a quantity of one).

The cart lists each line with its unit price, quantity, and line total, and a running subtotal. It also flags items that need attention:

  • Unavailable — the product is no longer active or is out of stock.
  • Only N left — low stock (5 or fewer remaining).

When you are ready, choose Proceed to checkout, or Continue shopping to keep browsing.

Updating quantities & removing items

  • Change a quantity — use the − / + buttons or type a number (1–99, never more than the stock on hand). The line total updates live as you type.
  • Update cart — saves all quantity changes at once.
  • Set a quantity to 0 — removes that item when you update.
  • Remove — takes a single line out of the cart immediately.
  • Clear cart — empties the whole cart (with a confirmation prompt).

Quantities are always re-checked on the server against current stock, so your subtotal stays accurate. If you request more than the available stock, the amount is reduced automatically and you are told.

Checkout

From the cart choose Proceed to checkout. Your cart must contain at least one item. The checkout form is pre-filled from your profile where possible, and collects:

  • Full name — required (up to 200 characters).
  • Phone number — required (up to 30 characters; digits and + ( ) - . and spaces).
  • Address line 1 — required; Address line 2 — optional.
  • City, Province / State, and Postal / ZIP code — required.
  • Payment method — see Payment methods.

The Order summary sidebar lists your items and subtotal. Choose Place order to continue. If any field needs fixing, the page reloads with a list of what to correct and your entries preserved.

Payment methods

Checkout is simulated — pick one of the following. No card numbers or CVV are ever collected:

  • Pay on pickup
  • Credit card (simulated — no real data collected)
  • Debit card (simulated — no real data collected)
  • PayPal (simulated)

The method you choose is stored as a label on the order so you can see how you intended to pay.

Order confirmation

When you place an order it is saved permanently and your cart is emptied. You are taken to a confirmation page that shows:

  • A unique confirmation number in the form CC-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX — keep it for your records.
  • Your shipping details and chosen payment method.
  • The items ordered, with quantities and a total.
  • The starting order status (Pending).

Prices are taken from a trusted server-side snapshot of your cart, not from the browser. From the confirmation you can open the full order details, go to your order history, or continue shopping.

Order history

Order history lists every order you have placed — and only your own orders. For each order you see its number, date, status, item count, payment method, and total, with a View link to the details.

Use the status filter (All, plus each status) to narrow the list. The summary line tells you how many orders match.

Order details

The order details page shows one order in full:

  • Current status and the date it was placed, plus the confirmation number.
  • A shipping details card and a payment & totals card (subtotal and total).
  • An items ordered table. Product lines link back to the product and list any chosen options; custom builds show an expandable list of build components with their prices.

You can only open your own orders. From here you can jump back to the confirmation, return to history, or continue shopping.

Order statuses

Every order moves through these statuses (an administrator updates them):

  • Pending — received; awaiting processing (the status of a new order).
  • Processing — being prepared or assembled.
  • Ready for pickup — ready to collect.
  • Completed — fulfilled.
  • Cancelled — the order was cancelled.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • “Your cart is empty” at checkout. — Add at least one item before checking out.
  • Quantity was reduced. — You asked for more than the stock available; the cart clamps to what is in stock.
  • Session expired message. — Reload the page and try again; this protects your cart and checkout against cross-site request forgery.
  • Can’t reach the cart or checkout. — These pages require you to log in.
  • Lost your confirmation number? — Open order history and select the order to see it again.
  • Item shows “Unavailable”. — It went out of stock or was withdrawn; remove it to continue to checkout.
