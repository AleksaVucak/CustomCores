Accounts & profile guide

This guide explains how to create a CustomCore account, sign in and out securely, read your profile dashboard, update your details, change your password, and what happens when a session times out or an account is disabled. Use the table of contents to jump to the task you are on.

Why create an account

You can browse the catalogue and use the PC Builder as a guest. An account lets you do more:

  • Save custom builds to your account and reopen them later.
  • Place orders at checkout and track them in your order history.
  • Request consultations and follow the responses.
  • Leave product reviews and keep a wishlist.
  • Manage your profile: name, contact details, and shipping address.

Create an account Log in

Creating an account

Open Create account from the navigation. If you are already logged in you will be sent to your profile instead, log out first to register a different account.

Fill in the form:

  • First name and Last name: required, up to 100 characters each.
  • Email address: required, must be a valid address, up to 255 characters, and not already registered.
  • Phone: optional. If provided it may contain digits and the characters + ( ) - . and spaces (up to 30 characters).
  • Password: required, at least 8 characters (up to 200).
  • Confirm password: must match the password exactly.

Choose Create account. When everything checks out you will see “Your account was created. You can now log in.” and be taken to the login page. New accounts are standard customer accounts and are active straight away.

If something is wrong, the form reloads with a message under each field and your entries preserved, except passwords, which are never echoed back. If the email is already registered you will be told, so log in instead if the account is yours.

Security note: your password is stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text, and it is never shown back to you.

Logging in

Open Log in, enter your email address and password, then choose Log in.

  • If your details are correct you will be greeted by name and taken to your account dashboard.
  • If you were sent to log in from another page (for example when saving a build or checking out), you will be returned to that page afterwards.
  • If the email or password is wrong you will see a single generic message: “Invalid email or password.” This is deliberate, it avoids revealing whether an email is registered.

For your security a fresh session is created every time you log in, so an old session cannot be reused.

Logging out

Choose Log out from the account menu. This clears your session completely and returns you to the login page with the message “You have been logged out.” After logging out, private pages such as your profile are no longer accessible until you sign in again.

Always log out on shared or public computers so the next person cannot reach your account.

Your profile dashboard

My account is your private home. It shows only your own information, data is always tied to the account you are signed in as, never to an address-bar id. It includes:

  • A welcome summary with your name, email, member-since date, and role.
  • Activity counts: orders, saved builds, wishlist items, consultations, and reviews.
  • Profile details: name, email, phone, and address (fields you have not filled in show as “Not set”).
  • Recent activity: your latest orders, saved builds, consultations, and reviews, with links where available.

Use the account navigation to reach Edit profile, or the buttons to browse the catalogue or open the PC Builder.

Editing your details

Open Edit profile. The page has two independent forms; this section covers Account details (see Changing your password for the other).

You can update:

  • First name and Last name: required, up to 100 characters each.
  • Email address: required and valid; it must not already be used by another account.
  • Phone: optional, same format as registration.
  • Address: optional address line 1 and 2, city, province, and postal code.

Choose Save details. On success you return to your profile with “Your profile details were updated.” If a field is invalid, or the email is taken by another account, the form reloads with a message and your entries kept. Choose Cancel to return without saving.

Changing your password

On Edit profile, use the Change password form:

  • Current password: required. You must confirm the password you use now.
  • New password: required, at least 8 characters (up to 200).
  • Confirm new password: must match the new password.

Choose Change password. If your current password is wrong you will be told and nothing changes. On success you return to your profile with “Your password was changed,” the new password is stored only as a secure hash, and your session is refreshed for safety, you stay logged in.

Staying signed in & session timeouts

CustomCore keeps you signed in with a secure session cookie, but sessions do not last forever. For your protection they end automatically when:

  • You are idle too long: after about 30 minutes without activity.
  • The session gets old: a single session lasts at most about 12 hours, even while active.

When a session ends this way you become a guest again. The next time you open a private page you will be asked to log in, and after signing in you are returned to where you were heading. Behind the scenes the session identifier is also rotated periodically to reduce the risk of session hijacking, you do not need to do anything for this.

Disabled accounts

An administrator can disable an account (for example for policy reasons). A disabled account cannot be used:

  • At login you will see “This account has been disabled. Please contact support,” and you will not be signed in.
  • If an account is disabled while you are already signed in, the next private page you open will log you out with a notice that the account is no longer available.

If you believe this is a mistake, please contact support so it can be reviewed.

Your data & privacy

Your profile shows only your own records, and account pages never accept another user’s id from the address bar. Passwords are stored as secure hashes and are never displayed. For details on how information is handled, see the privacy page.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • “Invalid email or password.”: Check the email spelling and that Caps Lock is off. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  • “An account with that email already exists.”: The email is registered; log in instead of registering.
  • Passwords do not match.: Retype both the password and confirmation fields carefully.
  • Signed out unexpectedly?: Your session likely timed out (see session timeouts). Just log in again.
  • Session expired message on a form.: Reload the page and submit again; this protects forms against cross-site request forgery.
  • Can’t change your email.: Another account may already use that address. Pick a different one or contact support.
  • Always log out on shared computers.
