## Trademark and brand statement

CustomCore artwork intentionally avoids readable CPU, GPU, motherboard, case,
operating-system, and retailer logos. Any generic hardware shapes are used only
to explain PC-building concepts. Names and trademarks belonging to real
technology companies are not presented as sponsors, partners, or product
endorsements. Product and system names in the catalogue (for example CoreStart,
ArenaPulse, ApexForge) are fictional labels created for this academic project.
